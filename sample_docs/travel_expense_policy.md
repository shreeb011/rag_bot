# Travel and Expense Policy

Effective 1 January 2025. Owner: Finance. Applies to all employees travelling on company business.

## Trip Approval

Domestic trips require approval from the employee's manager. International trips require approval from both the manager and the department head. Any trip, domestic or international, with a total estimated cost above USD 3,000 additionally requires approval from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

International trips must be booked at least 14 days before departure. Domestic trips should be booked at least 7 days before departure.

## Flights

Economy class is the standard for all flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights with a scheduled duration of 6 hours or more. Business class is permitted only with prior written approval from the CFO.

## Accommodation

The nightly hotel limit is INR 7,000 for domestic travel and USD 180 for international travel. In London, New York, Tokyo and Singapore, the international limit is USD 250 per night.

## Per Diem (Meals)

Employees receive a daily meal allowance (per diem) instead of submitting meal receipts. The domestic per diem is INR 2,000 per day and the international per diem is USD 60 per day. No per diem is paid for days on which all meals are provided by a conference, client or the company.

## Submitting Expense Claims

Expense claims must be submitted in ExpenseHub within 30 days of the end of the trip. Receipts are required for every individual expense above INR 500 (or USD 10 for international expenses). Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 business days of approval.

Claims submitted later than 30 days require approval from the Finance Director. Claims submitted more than 90 days after the end of the trip will not be reimbursed.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursed: alcohol, hotel minibar charges, personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, and seat or class upgrades that were not approved in advance.

## Lost Items While Travelling

Loss or theft of company equipment while travelling must be reported following the Information Security Policy. Personal items lost during a trip are not reimbursed by the company.
