# Nimbus Analytics Employee Handbook

Version 3.2, effective 1 March 2025. This handbook applies to all full-time employees of Nimbus Analytics Pvt. Ltd. in India. Contractors and interns are covered by their individual agreements.

## 1. Working Hours

The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 IST, during which employees are expected to be reachable. Outside core hours employees may choose a flexible start time between 08:00 and 11:00 IST.

Overtime is not expected as a routine practice. Work on a weekend or public holiday that is approved in advance by a manager earns one compensatory day off, which must be taken within 60 days.

## 2. Remote Work

Nimbus Analytics follows a hybrid model. Employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week. Tuesday is the company-wide team day, and all employees within commuting distance of an office are expected to work from the office on Tuesdays.

Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a Vice President. Working temporarily from another country is limited to 20 working days per calendar year and must be approved by HR at least 2 weeks in advance.

When working remotely, employees must follow the Information Security Policy, including its requirements for VPN use and device security.

## 3. Leave Policy

### 3.1 Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues at 2 days per completed month of service. Up to 10 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year; carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they lapse. Leave requests of 5 or more consecutive days should be submitted at least 3 weeks in advance.

### 3.2 Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days. Unused sick leave cannot be carried over or encashed.

### 3.3 Public Holidays

Nimbus Analytics observes 10 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar for the following year is published by HR every December.

### 3.4 Other Leave

Other leave types are governed by applicable local law and are not described in this handbook. Contact HR for details.

## 4. Equipment

Every employee is issued a company laptop (MacBook Pro 14 or Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon) on their first day. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.

New employees may claim a one-time home-office stipend of INR 25,000 for a chair, desk or monitor. The stipend must be claimed within the first 90 days of employment.

### 4.1 Lost or Damaged Equipment

Lost or stolen company equipment must be reported as described in the Information Security Policy. The first loss of a company device is not charged to the employee, provided it was reported within the required time. For any further loss within 24 months, the employee may be charged up to 50% of the device's depreciated value. Accidental damage is repaired at the company's cost.

## 5. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in April and October. Employees are rated on a 5-point scale. Salary revisions resulting from the April review take effect on 1 July.

## 6. Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 40,000 for courses, certifications, books and conferences. Spending requires prior manager approval. Unused learning budget does not roll over to the next year.
